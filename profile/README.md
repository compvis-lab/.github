We study nonlinear dynamical systems, integral equations and inverse problems, and apply them to forecasting, control and computer vision in power systems with renewable generation and storage.

The group is led by [Prof.&nbsp;Denis&nbsp;Sidorov](https://compvis.ru/people/denis-sidorov/) at the [Melentiev Energy Systems Institute SB&nbsp;RAS](https://isem.irk.ru/) and [Irkutsk National Research Technical University](https://www.istu.edu/).

We take PhD and MSc students, interns and postdocs, and you can write to us any time: [how to apply](https://compvis.ru/join/).
